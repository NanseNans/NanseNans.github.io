Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Sticks & Stones Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Sticks & Stones YouTube channel to manage the channel's own videos.

What data the tool uses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's videos, set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and subtitles, schedule when they are published, update the channel's banner and description, and post comments as the channel. It reads public comments on the channel's own videos and the channel's own view statistics (YouTube Analytics API) to decide what to make next.

What it does not do

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone. It does not access other people's accounts. Public comments it reads are only used to reply to them and to pick video ideas.

Google user data

Sticks & Stones Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen, or contact NanseNans on GitHub.
